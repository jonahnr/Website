Offering 02

Decision System Reset

A focused rebuild for teams whose reports look polished, but no longer align to the questions leadership actually needs answered.

See How The Reset Fits

Best Fit

Teams have dashboards, but decision-making still depends on explanation, debate, or manual reconciliation.

Typical Outcome

A simpler decision-ready reporting system with aligned metrics, governed logic, and clearer ownership.

Working Shape

A structured reset where we map decisions, redesign the core reporting logic, and guide the transition.

What Gets Reset

From Dashboard Volume To Decision Clarity

The work starts with the decisions leaders need to make, then rebuilds the analytics structure around those decisions.

  • Decision questions, operating rhythms, and leadership information needs.
  • Metric definitions, business rules, and semantic reporting standards.
  • Dashboards and reports that should be consolidated, retired, or rebuilt.
  • Ownership model for ongoing changes and metric governance.
  • Adoption plan so teams understand the new system and use it correctly.

What The Interaction Looks Like
