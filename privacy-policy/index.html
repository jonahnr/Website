Privacy Policy

How Parallax Data Lab handles information collected through this site.

This page explains what information may be collected through the Parallax Data Lab website, how it is used, and how to contact us with privacy questions.

Privacy overview

Last updated: June 10, 2026

01

Information you submit

If you complete a Fit Check form, contact form, email link, or scheduling link, Parallax Data Lab may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company, role, website, and context about your analytics needs.

02

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand page views, traffic sources, clicks, form interactions, and other website behavior. Analytics data helps improve the site, clarify service pages, and understand which content is useful.

03

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, evaluate whether Parallax Data Lab can help, schedule intro calls, improve the website, understand marketing performance, and prepare relevant follow-up communication.

04

Third-party tools

The site may use third-party services including Google Analytics, FormSubmit, Calendly, GitHub Pages, and email providers. Those services may process information according to their own privacy practices.

05

Information sharing

Parallax Data Lab does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when needed to operate the website, respond to your request, use connected service providers, comply with legal obligations, or protect the site and business.

06

Your choices

You can avoid submitting form information, use browser controls to limit cookies or analytics, and contact Parallax Data Lab to request that your submitted inquiry information be updated or deleted.

Questions about privacy?

Contact Parallax Data Lab with privacy questions or requests related to information you submitted through this site.
