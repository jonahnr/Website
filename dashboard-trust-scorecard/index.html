Self-assessment

Score the four places analytics trust usually breaks.

01

Metric trust

Leaders agree on what the KPI means, where it comes from, and when it is safe to use.

Weak signal: meetings debate the number before the decision.
02

Ownership

Every important metric has a business owner, logic owner, and decision owner.

Weak signal: no one can approve a definition change quickly.
03

Decision cadence

Reports are tied to recurring decisions, thresholds, and follow-up ownership.

Weak signal: dashboards are reviewed but action is unclear.
04

Operational signal

Leadership can see what changed, why it matters, and what needs attention next.

Weak signal: there is a lot of data but no weekly narrative.
