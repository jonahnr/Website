What it helps you find

A quick way to locate the first trust break.

The full working copy includes the rubric, per-question checklists, score scale, examples, evidence prompts, and interpretation guidance. This page only previews the five areas so the downloaded scorecard still does the actual diagnostic work.

01

Metric trust

Whether leaders can use priority KPIs without reopening definition debates.

02

Ownership

Whether the right people own definitions, interpretation, and follow-up.

03

Source reliability

Whether reports trace back to sources and refresh rules people trust.

04

Decision cadence

Whether dashboards connect to recurring decisions, thresholds, and action.

05

Operational signal

Whether leaders can see what changed, why it matters, and what needs attention.
