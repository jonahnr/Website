Facilities & Maintenance / anonymized case study

Facilities maintenance reporting made recurring risk and backlog easier to review.

Client or organization type
Facilities and maintenance operations team
Industry or operating environment
Work orders, backlog, compliance checks, asset condition, and recurring management review.
Original challenge
Backlog and risk reporting existed, but leaders could not quickly see what required intervention.
Work completed by Parallax
Designed a decision-focused backlog view, exception thresholds, owner notes, and operating cadence.
Results and outcomes
No verified quantitative client metrics available; outcomes are qualitative. Maintenance review became more explicit about priority, owner, and follow-up.
