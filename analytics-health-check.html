When a paid Health Check is worth scoping

This is for teams that need a clear read on the foundation before committing to a reset, retainer, or intelligence initiative.

01

Dashboards exist, but confidence is low

Leadership has reports, but the numbers still trigger side debates, manual validation, or second spreadsheets.

02

Metrics mean different things in different rooms

Definitions, ownership, and business logic have drifted enough that the same question gets multiple answers.

03

Reporting volume is high, but decisions are slow

The team is busy producing outputs, yet leaders still lack the clarity needed to act quickly.

04

Analytics ownership is unclear

Requests, priorities, and governance move through too many informal channels without a stable senior owner.
