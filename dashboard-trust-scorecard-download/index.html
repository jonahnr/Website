01Metric trust

What good looks like

Leaders agree on what the KPI means, where it comes from, and when it is safe to use.

Weak signal

Meetings debate the number before the decision.

Score metric trust
02Ownership

What good looks like

Every important metric has a business owner, logic owner, and decision owner.

Weak signal

No one can approve a definition change quickly.

Score ownership
03Source reliability

What good looks like

Priority reports trace back to dependable systems, documented transformations, and understood refresh rules.

Weak signal

Teams keep side spreadsheets because official reporting is not trusted.

Score source reliability
04Decision cadence

What good looks like

Reports are tied to recurring decisions, thresholds, and follow-up ownership.

Weak signal

Dashboards are reviewed, but action is unclear.

Score decision cadence

What good looks like

Weak signal

Interpretation guide

Use the pattern, not just the total.

Not sure which score matters most

Start with the Free Fit Check: a 15-minute routing conversation to identify the smallest useful next step.

Low metric trust or unclear definitions

Use the Analytics Health Check: a 1-2 week paid diagnostic that produces a foundation issue map and recommended next step.

Low source reliability

Use the Analytics Health Check to trace whether source systems, transformations, refresh rules, or report logic are creating the trust gap.

Low ownership or decision cadence

Use the Decision System Reset: a 4-8 week rebuild of decisions, metrics, owners, triggers, cadence, and escalation.

Scores keep drifting after cleanup

Use Fractional Analytics: ongoing senior ownership and governance that maintains and evolves the decision system.

Low operational signal quality

Use Intelligence Lab: advanced operational intelligence products, especially the Weekly Operational Intelligence Digest.

Mostly 4-5
