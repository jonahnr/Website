Offering 03

Fractional Analytics Consulting

Ongoing senior analytics support for teams that need judgment, structure, and ownership as metrics, priorities, and reporting needs continue to evolve.

See Where It Fits

Best Fit

Analytics has outgrown its original setup, but hiring a full-time analytics leader is premature or unclear.

Typical Outcome

Steadier analytics ownership, clearer standards, better prioritization, and fewer recurring trust debates.

Working Shape

An embedded advisory rhythm with recurring reviews, priority guidance, and targeted architecture support.

Where We Plug In

Senior Analytics Judgment Without Permanent Overhead

This is designed for teams that need ongoing structure and decision support without turning every analytics issue into a new project.

  • Recurring prioritization of analytics requests, reporting changes, and metric issues.
  • Guidance on data model, semantic layer, dashboard, and governance decisions.
  • Leadership-facing framing so analytics work stays connected to business value.
  • Support for internal teams as they implement standards and reduce reporting debt.
  • Ongoing stewardship as priorities, systems, and organizational ownership change.
