Free Analytics Fit Check

A free 15-minute meeting to find the right next step for your analytics problem.

Use the Fit Check when dashboards are not trusted, reporting volume is too high, KPI ownership is unclear, or operational signals are scattered across too many places. In 15 minutes, we clarify the friction and route you toward the smallest useful next step.

Recommended next step: scorecard, Analytics Health Check, Decision System Reset, Fractional Analytics, Intelligence Lab, or no engagement

When to use it

Start here when the issue is real, but the right engagement path is not obvious yet.

01

Dashboards exist, but confidence is low

Leadership still asks which number is right, whether the dashboard refreshed, or who adjusted the logic.

02

There is too much data and not enough signal

Reports, metrics, and operational updates are available, but leaders still lack a concise view of what needs attention.

03

KPI ownership is unclear

Metrics move across teams without a stable owner for definitions, interpretation, thresholds, or follow-up.

04

You may need a digest, reset, or senior analytics owner

The Fit Check determines whether the next step is diagnostic, strategic, ongoing, or simply not worth a paid engagement yet.

What happens in 15 minutes

The meeting is intentionally short, practical, and diagnostic.

Step 1

You describe the friction

Bring the messy version: dashboard distrust, reporting overload, metric disputes, slow decisions, or scattered operating signals.

No prep deck required.
Step 2

We identify the likely root cause

We separate dashboard symptoms from deeper issues in definitions, ownership, decision cadence, signal design, or analytics leadership.

The goal is clarity, not a sales pitch.
Step 3

You get a recommended path

The answer may be an Analytics Health Check, Decision System Reset, Fractional Analytics, Intelligence Lab initiative, or no engagement.

Paid work only follows if there is a clear fit.
