Our Offerings

Parallax Data Lab helps teams move from BI reporting confusion, dashboard debates, and metric drift to trusted decision systems. Start with diagnosis, rebuild the foundation when needed, or add senior analytics ownership as the business keeps changing.

Which path fits the problem in front of you?

The offerings are designed to meet teams at different stages of analytics maturity, from dashboard trust issues and metrics governance gaps to senior BI leadership needs. If you are unsure, start with the free fit check so the right path is scoped before any paid work begins.

01 Dashboards exist, but trust is shaky.

Start with a free fit check, then scope an Analytics Health Check if the main issue is trust, definition drift, duplicated logic, ownership gaps, or reporting friction.

 02 Reports no longer match how leaders make decisions.

Use a Decision System Reset to simplify metrics, rebuild core views, and align reporting around decision workflows.

 03 Analytics needs senior ownership over time.

Use Fractional Analytics Consulting when priorities keep shifting and your team needs ongoing standards, judgment, and cadence.

 04 You are ready for an intelligence initiative.

A premium path for predictive frameworks, governed intelligence layers, executive digests, and decision products once the foundation is stable.

How engagements work

Every path is built around the foundation beneath reporting.

Diagnose

Find what is breaking trust

We identify where definitions, data models, reporting logic, ownership, and decision flows are creating friction.

Design

Clarify the operating model

We define the standards, architecture, ownership, and decision logic needed for analytics to become dependable infrastructure.

Guide

Build the highest-leverage fix

We rebuild key assets directly or guide your team through the clearest sequence of changes.

Three examples of what the work can leave behind.

The final output depends on the Fit Check and paid scope. These examples show how Parallax turns a messy situation into a clear artifact, decision implication, and next move.

01Dashboard trust breakdown

Situation: leadership has multiple dashboards answering the same KPI differently.

Example deliverable
  • Metric and logic inventory showing where definitions split
  • Source-of-truth recommendation for the executive view
  • Fix sequence ranked by decision impact and implementation lift

02Decision review reset

Situation: weekly meetings are full of reporting updates, but decisions still stall.

Example deliverable
  • Decision inventory with owners, triggers, thresholds, and cadence

  • Monthly analytics operating rhythm and governance backlog
  • Definition maintenance plan for priority metrics and dashboards
  • Executive signal readout template for recurring decision support
