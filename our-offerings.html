Offering 01

Analytics Health Check

A focused diagnostic engagement for teams that need a clear view of where their analytics foundation is breaking down before investing in a larger rebuild.

  • Review dashboards, models, metrics, and reporting workflows.
  • Identify definition drift, duplicated logic, ownership gaps, and trust issues.
  • Produce a prioritized foundation roadmap with practical next steps.

What the interaction looks like

A short diagnostic sprint: we review your reporting environment, interview key stakeholders, identify trust breaks, and return a practical roadmap your team can act on.

Foundation health summary, issue map, ownership gaps, and recommended roadmap.

Offering 02

Decision System Reset

A rebuild engagement for teams that need to realign analytics around how leadership actually makes decisions.

  • Clarify the business questions dashboards must answer.
  • Redesign metric definitions and semantic reporting structure.
  • Refactor reporting assets into a simpler decision-ready system.

What the interaction looks like

A focused reset: we map decisions, simplify reporting logic, rebuild the core metrics and views, and help teams transition from dashboard volume to decision clarity.

Explore Decision System Reset

Best Fit

Reports are polished, but they no longer align to executive decisions.

Typical Output

Decision map, metric standards, streamlined reporting architecture, and governance recommendations.

Offering 03

Fractional Analytics Consulting

Ongoing senior analytics architecture support for teams that need judgment, structure, and ownership without immediately hiring a full-time analytics leader.

  • Provide recurring oversight of metrics, reporting standards, and decision logic.
  • Guide prioritization as new analytics needs emerge.
  • Support teams through scale, governance, and reporting evolution.

What the interaction looks like

An ongoing partnership: we join the operating rhythm, advise on analytics priorities, unblock structure decisions, and keep metrics, ownership, and reporting aligned as the business changes.

Explore Fractional Analytics

Best Fit

Analytics has outgrown its original setup, but a full-time analytics leadership hire is premature.

Typical Output

Ongoing standards, metric ownership, decision system refinement, and senior analytics guidance.
