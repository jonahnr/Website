Which path fits the problem in front of you?

The offerings are designed to meet teams at different stages of analytics maturity. If you are unsure, start with the Health Check.

01 Dashboards exist, but trust is shaky.

Start with an Analytics Health Check to diagnose definition drift, duplicated logic, ownership gaps, and reporting friction.

 02 Reports no longer match how leaders make decisions.

Use a Decision System Reset to simplify metrics, rebuild core views, and align reporting around decision workflows.

 03 Analytics needs senior ownership over time.

Use Fractional Analytics Consulting when priorities keep shifting and your team needs ongoing standards, judgment, and cadence.

 04 You are ready for an intelligence initiative.

Use Intelligence Lab when the foundation is ready for predictive frameworks, governed intelligence layers, or executive decision products.

How engagements work

Every path is built around the foundation beneath reporting.

Diagnose

Find what is breaking trust

Design

Guide

Build the highest-leverage fix

We rebuild key assets directly or guide your team through the clearest sequence of changes.
