Best fit

Who benefits most from Intelligence Lab work?

This is strongest for teams with a real decision problem, enough data to learn from, and a need for repeatable intelligence rather than another static report.

Leadership Teams With Complex Operating Signals

Teams managing risk, performance, churn, compliance, or operational variance across functions, sites, or regions.

Organizations Ready To Move Beyond Reporting

Teams that have dashboards in place but need prioritization, detection, forecasting, and guided action.

Analytics Teams That Need A Governed Intelligence Layer

Teams that need models, access rules, definitions, and executive outputs to hold together as the system grows.

How we build

Intelligence only works when the foundation is governed.

The Lab does not start by asking what model or dashboard to build. It starts by making the decision system explicit: what leaders need to know, which signals prove it, who owns the definitions, and how the insight turns into action.

01

Decision Question

Define the leadership decision first: what needs to be prioritized, predicted, escalated, or explained?

02

Signal Map

Identify the operational, customer, financial, workflow, or behavioral signals that actually indicate change.

03

Governed Logic

Align definitions, access rules, model ownership, and metric logic before automation makes the system louder.

04

Action Cadence

Design the digest, alert, framework, or executive view around who acts, when they act, and what changes next.

Example work

Example intelligence initiatives designed around specific decision problems.

These examples show how a clear leadership question can become governed data logic, delivery cadence, and an action path your team can run.

Operational intelligence

Fragmented Operations Intelligence Digest

Transforms disconnected operational signals into a ranked intelligence digest that shows leaders where attention is needed first.
  • Unifies signals from safety, service delivery, compliance, workflow participation, backlog, customer friction, and operational exceptions.
  • Detects escalation trends, ownership gaps, stalled actions, regional variance, capacity strain, and early signs of instability.
  • Best when leaders have operational data everywhere but lack a clear system for prioritizing what needs action now.

Governance architecture

Enterprise Governance & RLS Architecture

Creates secure, role-aware analytics architecture with governed models, identity mapping, and scalable access rules.
  • Connects identity, permissions, dynamic row-level security, certified semantic models, and reporting layers.
  • Supports enterprise analytics environments where trust depends on consistent logic and controlled access.
  • Best when analytics needs to scale securely without fragmenting across teams or workspaces.

Outcome systems

Enterprise Outcome Studio

Connects governance, performance improvement, reporting standardization, and automation into a measurable outcome system.
  • Targets manual reporting reduction, performance improvement, metric consistency, and governed access.
  • Frames analytics transformation around outcomes rather than disconnected technical deliverables.
  • Best when leadership needs a clearer operating model for scalable analytics improvement.

Customer intelligence

Customer Health & Churn Intelligence

Identifies deteriorating account health, renewal risk, operational friction, and expansion opportunities earlier.
  • Combines engagement signals, behavioral patterns, predictive churn modeling, and retention prioritization.
  • Creates a leadership view of which accounts need attention and why the risk is emerging.
  • Best when customer success and leadership need proactive retention intelligence, not retrospective account review.

Boundaries

What Intelligence Lab intentionally avoids.

Clear boundaries keep advanced intelligence work focused on decision systems, not endless reporting requests.

We do not operate as a ticket-based reporting team.

We do not replace the primary data engineering function of record.

We do not rebuild upstream ETL as part of standard intelligence work.

We do not automate decisions until definitions, owners, and action paths are clear.
